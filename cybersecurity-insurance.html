Potential Coverage Areas

Financial Protection for Cyber Events

Cyber policies commonly combine first-party expenses with third-party liabilities. Exact coverage, limits, deductibles, conditions, and exclusions vary by carrier and policy.

info

Insurance products, where available, are offered through appropriately licensed insurance partners. Coverage is subject to underwriting and the terms of the issued policy.

database

Data Recovery

Costs to restore data and systems damaged, destroyed, or stolen in a covered event.

encrypted

Cyber Extortion

Response expenses associated with ransomware and other covered extortion demands.

notifications

Breach Response

Potential notification, forensic investigation, legal, credit monitoring, and crisis costs.

gavel

Cyber Liability

Potential network security, privacy, media, and regulatory liabilities arising from a covered incident.

Risk-Informed Approach

Security Work That Supports Insurance Readiness

  1. 01

    Understand the Business

    Review revenue, operations, sensitive data, dependencies, prior incidents, and the financial impact of downtime.

  2. 02

    Assess Technical Risk

    Test external and internal systems to identify weaknesses that automated questionnaires and perimeter scans can miss.

  3. 03

    Reduce Exposure

    Prioritize remediation by exploitability and business impact, then verify that material weaknesses are resolved.

  4. 04

    Maintain the Evidence

    Use continuous monitoring and reporting to keep the security posture current through underwriting and renewal cycles.
