Six Operational Domains

See Maturity Across the Whole Security Program

The questionnaire moves from leadership and risk decisions through technical safeguards and incident operations.

corporate_fare

Security Governance

Examine oversight, policies, roles, awareness, and the processes used to sustain the security program.

warning

Security Risk Management

Assess how cyber risks are identified, evaluated, treated, tracked, and communicated across the organization.

shield_lock

Data Protection

Review the safeguards and operating practices used to manage and protect sensitive information.

manage_accounts

Access Management

Evaluate how identities, privileges, authentication, and access lifecycles are governed and controlled.

architecture

Security Architecture

Measure operational controls across infrastructure, endpoints, applications, monitoring, and protective technologies.

emergency

Incident Response

Assess readiness, detection, containment, investigation, remediation, and the use of incident metrics.

From Response to Insight

More Context Than a One-Size-Fits-All Checklist

The assessment combines structured choices with contextual follow-ups. Depending on the topic and response, TAMS can ask about frequency, scope, ownership, process detail, supporting technology, or the extent of implementation.

fact_check

Built Around Real Operations

Questions cover both documented controls and how those controls are implemented, reviewed, managed, and measured in practice.
